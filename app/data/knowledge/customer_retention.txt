Customer Retention Policy

Customer churn often increases when customers experience repeated delivery delays, unresolved support issues, or poor service quality.

Customers who submit frequent support tickets are at higher risk of cancelling their subscriptions if issues remain unresolved.

Retention can improve when high-risk customers receive proactive outreach, personalized support, and faster issue resolution.

Premium customers tend to remain more loyal when support response times stay within agreed service-level targets.

Customer satisfaction is strongly influenced by the consistency of service delivery and the quality of support interactions.
